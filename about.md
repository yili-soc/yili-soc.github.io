I'm a senior SOC analyst with over 10 years of experience in security operations, based in Calgary, Canada.

My work centers on threat detection and incident response: triaging escalations, writing detection rules, and building response playbooks. I've worked with Microsoft Sentinel, Defender, Splunk and QRadar, mostly in financial-sector and multi-tenant (MSSP) environments. I hold the Microsoft SC-200 certification.

## What this blog is about

I attend local security conferences such as BSides and write up the talks from a SOC analyst's point of view. Talks usually explain how an attack works. My posts try to answer the next question: how would you actually detect and respond to it in your own environment?

Each post covers:

- The attack and its public sources
- The detection logic, with KQL queries
- Response steps, and what is worth automating

## Disclaimer

All views are my own and do not represent any employer. Incident details come from public sources, which are linked in each post.
