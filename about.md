I'm **Yi Li**, a senior SOC analyst with over 10 years of experience in security operations, based in Calgary, Canada.

My work centers on threat detection and incident response: triaging escalations, writing detection rules, and building response playbooks. I also spent several years in product management at Forcepoint and Alibaba Cloud Security, and I'm interested in how security products are designed.

I believe community is one of the foundations of cybersecurity. Attackers already share tools, access, and techniques with each other. Defenders can do the same: build trust across organizations and share what we learn. That's why I go to security conferences, volunteer at local events, and share my own work here.
